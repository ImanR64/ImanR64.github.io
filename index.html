Example: RiboSort

Counting ribosomal subunits by eye in hundreds of negative-stain EM micrographs is slow and hard to reproduce. I built RiboSort, a desktop tool on the napari platform. It detects particles, sorts them into classes with adjustable size and distance rules, learns from a few hand-labelled micrographs, and exports a counts table for every dataset.

The same approach works for other particle types and imaging data. Your team gets a tool it owns and can rerun, not a one-off result.
